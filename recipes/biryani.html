Biryani Recipe

This is a very basic Biryani recipe. It is an Indian meat and rice based dish.

Ingredients

  • Basmati Rice
  • Garam Masala
  • Sliced Chicken
  • Yoghurt
  • Onion and Garlic
  • Ghee
  • Lemon Juice

Preparation

  1. Make the marinade by mixing yogurt, ginger, garlic, turmeric, red chili powder, garam masala, salt, mint leaves, and lemon juice.
  2. Add chicken and coat evenly with the marinade. Keep in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes. You can marinate the chicken overnight too.
  3. While the chicken is marinating, rinse and drain the rice 2 to 3 times, then soak in water for 20 minutes.
  4. While the rice is soaking, add ghee to a heavy-bottomed pan and add onions.
  5. On medium heat, fry the onions stirring frequently until they start to turn golden brown - about 15 to 20 minutes.
  6. Remove the fried onions from the pan, leaving the excess ghee behind as we will use this pan to cook the chicken.
  7. Top with rice with caramelized onions and saffron-infused milk.
  8. Cover the pot and seal it with aluminum foil, then place some weight on top of the lid.
  9. Cook on low heat for 20 minutes, this is the steam or "Dum" cooking step.
  10. Turn the heat off and wait another 10 minutes before opening the pot.
